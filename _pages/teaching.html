Teaching Philosophy

I didn't have a smooth educational journey myself, but I never wanted to give up because I've always been curious and wanted to test things out for myself rather than simply accept what others say or think. I often think about what more I can do in education.

I believe everyone is smart in different ways, and I'm here to help others succeed and learn something new from me. I don't usually use the word "teaching" because I see it more as sharing everything I've learned so far with others. Everyone can take what I share, test whether it holds true, and explore alternative perspectives based on their own experiences.

Discussing what we're passionate about and what we're working on is especially meaningful to me because it allows me to learn from other people's perspectives as well.
