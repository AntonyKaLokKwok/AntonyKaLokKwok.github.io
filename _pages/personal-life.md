Academic work is only one part of my life. Before pursuing economics, mathematics, and urban planning, sports and athletics were major parts of my childhood and teenage years in Hong Kong.

Growing up, I participated in many different school sports teams, competitions, and athletic activities throughout primary and secondary school. These experiences allowed me to explore different interests, challenge myself, build friendships, and learn the importance of teamwork, discipline, and perseverance.

## Sports and Athletics

### Karate — Hong Kong National Team

*2008–2014*

Karate was one of the most important parts of my childhood and teenage years. I was selected for the Hong Kong karate national team and participated in extensive training, domestic competitions, and international tournaments.

I represented Hong Kong at junior and senior competitive levels, earning placements in international and regional karate competitions.

Some of my achievements include:

- **2013:** Third Place (Junior and Senior Teams), 9th Korea Open International Karatedo Championships.
- **2013:** Third Place (Junior Team), Hong Kong Karate Kata Team Tournament Championships.
- **2012:** Winner, 6th Karate Friendship Competition.

My karate experiences helped shape my discipline, determination, and willingness to challenge myself.

For more about my early life and athletic journey, see [My Journey](/my-journey/).
