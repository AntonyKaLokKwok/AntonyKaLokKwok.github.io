My research brings together **urban economics, housing policy, transportation accessibility, geographic information systems (GIS), and spatial econometrics**. I am interested in how urban policies shape neighborhood conditions and access to opportunity.

## Research Areas

- Affordable housing, housing policy, and neighborhood change
- Transportation accessibility and spatial inequality
- Urban environmental inequality and climate-related risks
- Urban informatics and digital inequality

## Selected Research Projects

These project pages will be expanded with research questions, data, methods, figures, and findings.

### Affordable Housing and Neighborhood Outcomes

Research on subsidized housing, neighborhood conditions, and spatial spillovers.

### Bluebikes Accessibility and Transportation Equity

Spatial analysis of bike-share accessibility, affordable housing, and transportation disadvantage in Greater Boston.

### Urban Heat and Spatial Inequality

Quantitative and GIS-based analysis of uneven urban heat exposure.

### Digital Urban Inequality

Research on how digital information and urban representation vary across places.

### GIS and Spatial Analysis Projects

A space for course-based maps, spatial analysis, and methodological projects, including work on Greater Boston and Grand Annecy.
