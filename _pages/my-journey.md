# My Journey

## Growing Up in Hong Kong

My experiences growing up in Hong Kong
helped shape my understanding of housing,
inequality, and access to opportunity.

## From Athletics to Academia

My journey from athletics to academic
research has shaped my determination
and approach to learning.

## My Academic Journey

As a first-generation university student,
I studied economics, mathematics, and
statistics at the University of Minnesota
before continuing my graduate education
at Tufts University.

## Why I Study Cities

My personal experiences motivate my interest
in understanding how housing policies,
urban environments, and spatial inequality
affect people's opportunities.
